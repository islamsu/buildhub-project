--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## Two pre-existing defects found by probing the source
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**1. `awaitingRenewalSync` granted paid access indefinitely.** I executed the
real function rather than trusting the 4B.1 report:

    1 day after period end    effectivePlan=premium  isPaid=true
    10 years after            effectivePlan=premium  isPaid=true

An `active` row whose paid period ended a decade ago still resolved to full
PREMIUM — unlimited qualified enquiries, forever. This is exactly what §6 said
must not happen. Paid access is now bounded by `RENEWAL_SYNC_WINDOW_DAYS`, which
**reuses the approved 7-day grace period rather than inventing a second number**
— both windows answer the same question ("we don't know whether this vendor
paid"). Past the bound it fails closed to FREE and reports
`RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`, **preserving the row** so a late provider event can
still settle it. Neither success nor failure is invented.

**2. `startTrial` wiped the founder one-time-use stamp.** It emitted
`founderPriceUsedAt: null` for non-founder subscriptions, so a vendor who spent
the offer, cancelled, and re-subscribed became founder-eligible again on the next
cycle. Caught by a test I wrote for §16, confirmed live in scenario M.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## A hole I nearly introduced, and closed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`startPaidTrial` required `!isPaid` — but a lapsed trial leaves a vendor unpaid,
so they could chain **unlimited free 30-day paid-plan trials**. Closed with a
write-once `trialStartedAt` column (additive migration 0016). I've flagged "one
trial per vendor" as a rule I made explicit, not one I was handed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## A scope judgment worth your attention
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I did **not** expose plan selection to vendors. No payment can be collected until
4B.5, so `billing.startTrial` / `billing.changePlan` would have handed out real
paid entitlements — including PREMIUM's unlimited enquiries — for nothing. The
transitions are built and fully tested; they're admin-authorized until checkout
exists. Vendors get exactly two mutations, `cancelSubscription` and
`resumeSubscription`, both taking **no input at all**.
