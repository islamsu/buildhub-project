================================================================================

  NO. Read from the workflow triggers themselves:

    deploy-staging.yml      on: push: branches: [main]   +  workflow_dispatch

  So a merge to main starts the STAGING deploy and Render's autoDeploy of the
  main tip. Production cannot start from a merge; it requires a human to
  dispatch it, and it takes an image tag that the staging deploy must have
  published first.

  STAGING_SSH_KEY exist - it checks for them and skips with a notice rather
